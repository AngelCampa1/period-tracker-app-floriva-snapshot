# Floriva 1.3.0 Billing Evidence

Release identity: iOS `1.3.0 (21)` and Android `1.3.0 (21)`, bundle/package
`app.floriva`.

This record separates live configuration checks from native transaction
evidence. A product, base-plan, offer, or artifact audit is not represented as a
purchase, restore, crossgrade, offer-redemption, or ownership transaction.

## Release decision

The release owner explicitly confirmed the final Apple submission and Google
Play 100% production-rollout submission on 2026-07-24. Missing physical-device
Liquid Glass sign-off and missing build-21 native billing transactions were
accepted as release exceptions. They remain `not run` or `blocked`; they are not
implied passes.

No subscriber migration, price migration, subscription-group move, base-plan
replacement, or entitlement rewrite is part of 1.3.0.
